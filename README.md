# ImageLocate — public documents

The support page, privacy policy, and terms of use for the ImageLocate iOS app,
published so App Store Connect and the app itself have public URLs to point at.

| Page | URL |
|---|---|
| Support | https://aerologefb.github.io/imagelocate-app/ |
| Privacy policy | https://aerologefb.github.io/imagelocate-app/privacy.html |
| Terms of use | https://aerologefb.github.io/imagelocate-app/terms.html |

The app links these pages from Settings → About (`ImageLocate/App/AppInfo.swift` in the
private app repository). Keep the privacy policy in step with what the app actually sends:
if a new online service is added to the app, add it to the table in `privacy.html`.
